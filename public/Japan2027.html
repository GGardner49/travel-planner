Making the Case
Why Japan? Why Now?
🍁
Fall Foliage Season
October is peak koyo — Japan's fall color season. Temples, bamboo groves, and mountain paths in full red and gold.
👶
Perfect Age for Cora
She'll be almost 2 — old enough to experience the magic, young enough to fly as a lap infant for free.
🛡️
Safest Country on Earth
Famously clean, punctual, and welcoming. One of the easiest countries in the world to travel with a baby.
🎡
Tokyo DisneySea
Ranked #1 theme park in the world. Nothing like the US parks — a completely different, magical experience.
🍜
The Best Food on Earth
Ramen, wagyu, sushi, yakitori, matcha everything. Japan has more Michelin stars than any country in the world.
🏯
Living History
Ancient shrines and bullet trains side by side. The contrast of old and new is unlike anywhere else.

Days 1 – 4
Tokyo
Day 1
🛬
Arrive + Settle In
Base in Daikanyama or Ebisu — one of Tokyo's most charming, walkable neighborhoods. Easy first night to decompress and explore.
Day 2
⛩️
Shrines + Streets
Meiji Shrine in the morning → quick Harajuku walk → Shibuya Crossing → wander back through Daikanyama. Dinner in the neighborhood.
Day 3 — Highlight ⭐
🎡
Tokyo DisneySea
Not your average Disney. Seven fantasy "ports," world-class rides, incredible food. Arrive at rope drop — leave mid-afternoon before the rush.
Day 4
🗻
Hakone Day Trip
Train to Hakone for the volcanic ropeway over Owakudani, Lake Ashi views of Mt. Fuji, and an optional onsen soak. Back to Tokyo by evening.
🏯
Days 5 – 8
Kyoto — The Ancient Capital
Day 5
🚅
Bullet Train Arrival
Shinkansen from Tokyo at 200 mph — 2.5 hrs. Arrive in the afternoon. Evening stroll through Gion, Kyoto's famous geisha district.
Day 6
⛩️
Kyoto's Greatest Hits
Fushimi Inari gates at sunrise (go early — it's magical). Nishiki Market for snacks. Kiyomizudera temple. Higashiyama old street.
Day 7
🍵
Uji — Japan's Tea Capital
Short train to Uji. Matcha everything — lattes, ice cream, sweets. Byodo-in Temple (the image on the 10-yen coin). Riverside walk.
Day 8
🎍
Arashiyama Bamboo Grove
The iconic bamboo forest at dawn — then Monkey Park, the Oi River trail, and the best tofu lunch of your life.
The Real Reason We're Going
The Food Is Unreal
🍜
Ramen
Tokyo-style, Kyoto-style, from tiny 8-seat shops at midnight. Every bowl is different. You'll never think about ramen the same way.
🍣
Sushi + Seafood
Tsukiji outer market for breakfast. Conveyor belt sushi that's somehow incredible. Omakase for a special night.
🍡
Street Food
Takoyaki, yakitori, mochi, taiyaki. Every corner is a snack. Nishiki Market in Kyoto alone could fill a whole afternoon.
🍵
Matcha Everything
Matcha lattes, ice cream, Kit-Kats, traditional ceremony in Uji — the birthplace of Japanese tea. Cora will try it all.
🥩
Wagyu
Japanese beef is in a category of its own. Pick one night for Kobe or Matsusaka wagyu — it's worth every yen.
🍺
Izakayas
Japanese pub-style dining — beer, sake, small plates, late nights. The best way to end any day in Tokyo or Kyoto.
